Product overview

Product description

Automate your entire document workflow directly in Creatio.

PandaDoc Document Management for Creatio helps sales teams create, send, track, and sign documents without leaving CRM. From proposals to contracts, the entire document lifecycle is fully automated — saving time, reducing manual work, and accelerating deal closure.

Generate documents in a few clicks using standardized templates shared across your team. Customer data is automatically pulled from Creatio, eliminating copy-paste errors and ensuring accuracy in every proposal.

Track document progress in real time: views, comments, approvals, and status updates are instantly available inside Creatio. Recipients can review and complete documents digitally using secure, unlimited eSignatures included in every PandaDoc subscription.

By connecting PandaDoc with Creatio, businesses streamline document operations, shorten sales cycles, and improve overall sales performance.

Key Features

Enjoy document management within Creatio CRM

Facilitate sales process by reducing time spent on creating documents. The relevant data from the CRM such as contacts, products, pricing or any other data are automatically populated in the documents. eSign in PandaDoc, track signature status in Creatio.

Installation

Setup guide

  1. Actualize CSP settings. Run the 'Actualize CSP header' business process.
  2. Log into PandaDoc portal. Go to "Opportunity" / "Leads" or "Accout" section, open record and go to "PANDADOC" tab. Click “CONNECT”. PandaDoc login page will open in a new tab. Log in and refresh the Creatio page.
  3. Configure settings. Go to "System designer" > "PandaDoc Settings" and setup fields should be populated in PandaDoc. You can work with the following settings: Opportunities, Lead, Account. Each of these settings represents the list of Creatio fields that is populated in PandaDoc.
