Overview

Product overview

Product description

With the app, you can easily create leads from visually stunning landing pages, or import from file, and streamline your lead and opportunity workflows with automation.

Use cases:

  • Lead and Opportunity Management has all the right tools for capturing and tracking leads through a broad range of sources. Add leads from different sources into the single database in just a few clicks. Let Sales Creatio automatically verify the data, create a new contact and guide you through the process of lead management.
  • Manage all opportunities using the out-of-the-box business process. Define tactics for each opportunity, identify key decision makers, track competitors, analyze their strengths and weaknesses. Generate sales forecasts taking into account client’s budget, opportunity stage and probability.
  • Regardless of the complexity and size of your product portfolio, be certain you can store them all in.

Key Features

Lead and Opportunity Summarization AI Skills

Lead and Opportunity Summarization AI Skills: Provides concise summaries of leads or opportunities, including status, customer needs, products of interest, and recent interactions. This feature aids sales representatives in quickly understanding and prioritizing leads

Sales Process Automation

Provides tools to define tactics for each opportunity, identify key decision-makers, track competitors, and analyze their strengths and weaknesses

Product Management

Allows tracking and management of products associated with leads and opportunities to drive more accurate and efficient sales

MEDDPICC Qualification

Qualifies every opportunity against the full MEDDPICC framework — Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria and process, Paper process, Identified pain, Champion, and Competition. Reps score each element, capture the rationale, and set the next step, giving the team a consistent read on deal health before a deal is committed to the forecast.

Sales Performance and Operations

Enhances sales performance by tracking key metrics and optimizing team activities

Customer 360° Profile

Provides a comprehensive view of each customer and account, enabling efficient lead and opportunity management

Sales Pipeline Inspection

Understand pipeline movement over a period — what drove the change in totals, and how deals moved between Commit, Best Case, and other categories.

Manual app installation

  1. Open the needed app → the Packages tab - download the Markeplace app files.
  2. Click in the top right → Application Hub
  3. Click New application. This opens a window.
  4. Select Install from file and click Select in the window that opens.
  5. Click Select file on the page of the Marketplace App Installation Wizard and specify the app file path.
