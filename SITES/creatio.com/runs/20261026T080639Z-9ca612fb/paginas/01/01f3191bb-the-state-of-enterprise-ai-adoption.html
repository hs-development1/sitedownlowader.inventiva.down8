About

The State of Enterprise
AI Adoption

The State of Enterprise AI Adoption Report is Creatio’s second annual research study examining how business and technology leaders are approaching AI and AI agents. The study surveyed 608 IT and business decision-makers, covering AI adoption, agent deployment, workforce impact, business outcomes, governance, barriers to scale and AI value.
